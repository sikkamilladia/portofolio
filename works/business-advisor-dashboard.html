Business Advisor Dashboard

2024 · Mini Project · Python, Pandas, NumPy, Streamlit, Plotly

Overview

A dashboard for visualizing business metrics and providing actionable insights. It helps stakeholders monitor performance and spot growth opportunities by turning static sales records into interactive charts.

What I did

  • Built KPI tracking for total revenue, average order value and profit margin.
  • Wrote data-cleaning scripts that handle missing values and inconsistent formats.
  • Added interactive filters by region, time period and product category.
  • Highlighted top-performing products and unusual sales patterns with visual cues.

Result

A working Streamlit dashboard that turns raw sales data into KPIs and interactive visuals. I learned how important data cleaning is before any chart can be trusted.
