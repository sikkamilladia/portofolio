Paper Lantern Poster Series

2025 · Designer · Illustrator, risograph

Overview

A set of three posters announcing a campus lantern festival. Each poster uses two spot colors so the series reads as one family.

What I did

  • Drew the lantern shapes by hand and traced them in vector form.
  • Prepared color separations for a two-color risograph print.
  • Printed and trimmed 60 copies per poster.
